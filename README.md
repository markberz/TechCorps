## 🧠 What does C.O.R.P.S. stand for?
At Tech Corps, we don’t just look at the price tag—we look at the soul of the machine. We use the **C.O.R.P.S. framework** to ensure you get the best value for your investment:

- **C - CPU (Processor)**: The heart of the machine. We only offer processors (Intel i5/i7/i9 or Ryzen 5/7/9) that can handle intense rendering and fast-paced gaming.
- **O - OS (Operating System)**: Fully optimized and licensed Windows 11 environments, ready for professional work and the latest security updates.
- **R - RAM (Memory)**: High-speed multitasking. Our standard starts at 16GB, ensuring zero lag even when you have dozens of tabs and heavy apps open.
- **P - Ports & Portability**: We ensure you have the right connectivity (HDMI, USB-C, Thunderbolt) and the right build quality, whether it's a sleek laptop or a beastly desktop rig.
- **S - Storage (SSD)**: Speed is non-negotiable. We strictly provide high-speed NVMe SSDs for near-instant boot times and rapid file transfers.
